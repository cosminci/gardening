### Phasing Recommendation

**Phase 1 — Start with Tier 1 only + existing baby plants.** This is the core setup. ~60-70% of the budget.

**Phase 2 — Add Tier 2 in year two** once you've observed how the microclimate behaves across one full summer + winter cycle. The first year teaches more than any guide.
