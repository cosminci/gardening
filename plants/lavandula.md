# English Lavender (*Lavandula angustifolia*)

**Tier:** 1 | **Category:** Perennial | **Survival estimate:** 85% | **Quantity:** 3

## Placement
Full sun — the hotter the better. Thrives in the most exposed, baked positions on the terrace.

## Pot Setup

| | Type | Volume | Dimensions (approx.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inner pot** | Plastic | 8L | ~24cm Ø × 20cm H |
| **Outer pot** | Rigid, light-colored, no drain holes | 12L | ~28cm Ø × 24cm H |

**System:** Shared tray + capillary mat OR individual small double-wall pots

## Soil Mix — Group B: Mediterranean

> **CRITICAL:** Standard potting mix killed the previous lavender. This plant requires POOR, GRITTY, FAST-DRAINING soil. Group B is non-negotiable.

| Component | Ratio | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| Kekkilä universal | 40% | 2.1L |
| Perlite / pumice | 30% | 1.6L |
| Coarse sand / gravel | 20% | 1.0L |
| LECA (in mix) | 10% | 0.5L |
| **Total soil mix** | | **5.2L** |

> **Simplified alternative:** 60% potting soil + 40% perlite/gravel if the full mix is not available.

### Pot layers (bottom to top)
1. **Drainage** — LECA: 1.2L
2. **Transition** — pure perlite: 0.4L
3. **Soil mix**: 5.2L
4. **Mulch** — 2-3cm gravel (keeps crown dry, reflects heat, prevents crown rot)

## Watering
- **Summer:** Only when soil is fully dry. Every 4-5 days typically — but always check before watering.
- **Winter:** Every 3-4 weeks at most. NEVER let it sit in water. Keep almost completely dry.

## Feeding
- Minimal. Light slow-release granular in March only. Lavender prefers poor soil — overfeeding produces lush growth that is more frost-susceptible.

## Winter Care
- *Lavandula angustifolia* is hardy to -20°C **IF the soil is dry**. Wet roots + freezing temperatures = death.
- Keep almost completely dry through winter. This is the single most important care instruction.
- Wrap pot to protect roots.

## Additional Notes
- **SPECIES MATTERS:** Must buy *Lavandula angustifolia* specifically. Recommended cultivars: 'Hidcote' or 'Munstead'.
- **DO NOT buy** French lavender (*L. stoechas*) — dies below -5°C and is not suitable for this climate.
- Previous lavender death was likely caused by: overwatering, wrong soil mix, or wrong species.
- Never allow pot to sit in a saucer of water at any time of year.
- Trim lightly after flowering (August) to maintain compact shape — do not cut into old wood.
- ×3 plants in a row creates a classic, fragrant Mediterranean edge along the sunniest aspect.
