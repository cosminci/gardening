# Clematis (*Clematis* spp.)

**Tier:** Existing baby | **Category:** Climber | **Survival estimate:** 50% | **Quantity:** 1

## Placement

"Cool feet, warm head" — the pot must sit in shade (shaded by other pots, a wall, or permanent shade cast by larger plants), while the stems and foliage climb upward into sun or partial sun. This is not optional. On a S-W 12th-floor balcony, pot soil in direct sun reaches 55°C in summer; clematis roots die above 30°C. The placement configuration is the primary survival factor for this plant.

## Pot Setup

| | Type | Volume | Dimensions (approx.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inner pot** | Fabric (dense fibrous roots benefit from air pruning; improves drought resilience) | 20L DEEP (~Ø30 × H30 cm) | ~Ø30 × H30 cm |
| **Outer pot** | Rigid, light-colored, no drain holes | 35L | ~Ø40 × H33 cm |

**System:** Double-wall with reservoir — MANDATORY. The air gap in the double-wall system provides critical insulation against soil overheating. Without this, root zone temperatures will exceed lethal thresholds.

## Soil Mix — Group C: Moisture-loving

| Component | Ratio | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| Kekkilä universal | 50% | 6.5L |
| Perlite | 20% | 2.6L |
| Composted bark | 15% | 1.95L |
| LECA | 10% | 1.3L |
| Vermiculite | 5% | 0.65L |
| **Total soil mix** | | **13L** |

### Pot layers (bottom to top)
1. **Drainage** — LECA: 3L
2. **Transition** — pure perlite: 1L
3. **Soil mix**: 13L
4. **Mulch** — 7–8 cm bark mulch (extra-thick — root temperature control is critical)

## Watering

Consistent moisture — the #1 killer is the combination of root overheating and drought. Daily in summer, without exception. The reservoir in the double-wall system provides a buffer but must be checked and refilled daily in peak heat. Every 2–3 days in spring and autumn. Weekly in winter.

## Feeding

High-potash feed (tomato fertilizer) every 2 weeks during the active flowering period. This supports flower production rather than excessive leafy growth.

## Winter Care

Most clematis species are cold-hardy to -20°C or below. Wrap pot in bubble wrap November–March. Top growth may die back partially or completely — this is normal. Do not cut back in autumn; prune only in spring once new growth is visible and you can confirm which stems are alive.

## Additional Notes

**WHY THIS PLANT IS HIGH-RISK:** The fundamental challenge is root temperature on a S-W 12th-floor balcony. Without the double-wall pot system plus heavy mulch plus shade placement for the pot, this plant will almost certainly fail in the first summer. All three protections are required simultaneously.

The deep pot is mandatory — clematis roots grow downward, not outward. A standard shallow container restricts root development and accelerates root overheating. The Ø30 × H30 cm dimension is the minimum acceptable.

**If the plant dies back to ground level** — from winter, summer stress, or unknown causes — do not discard. Many clematis regenerate vigorously from the crown and root system. Wait until June before concluding the plant is dead. New shoots from the base are a reliable sign of survival.

**Trellis or wires are required.** Clematis climbs by wrapping leaf stalks around supports — it needs thin supports (wires, bamboo, fine trellis) rather than thick posts. Tie in only the first stems; established stems self-attach. Secure all growth against the 12th-floor wind — unsecured growth will be stripped.

Success in year one with no wilt and no root die-back significantly improves the long-term prognosis. If it survives its first Bucharest summer in this configuration, it is likely to establish permanently.
