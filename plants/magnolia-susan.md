# Magnolia 'Susan' (*Magnolia* × *Susan*)

**Tier:** Existing baby | **Category:** Deciduous tree | **Survival estimate:** 60% | **Quantity:** 1

## Placement

Morning sun only, afternoon shade — position in the transition zone of the Z-shape where full S-W afternoon sun does not reach. Full afternoon sun scorches and desiccates the young, delicate leaves. The most wind-sheltered available corner is mandatory. This is the single most critical placement decision for this plant.

## Pot Setup

| | Type | Volume | Dimensions (approx.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inner pot** | Fabric (air pruning + easy repotting) | 30L | ~Ø38 × H32 cm |
| **Outer pot** | Rigid, light-colored, no drain holes | 50L | ~Ø46 × H38 cm |

**System:** Double-wall with reservoir. The fabric pot bottom must NOT touch the reservoir water — raise on 3–4 cm tall spacers (e.g. LECA balls or pot feet) to maintain an air gap beneath the fabric.

### Pot layers (bottom to top)
1. **Drainage** — LECA: 4.5L
2. **Transition** — pure perlite: 1.5L
3. **Soil mix**: 19.5L
4. **Mulch** — 5–6 cm bark mulch (moisture retention critical)

## Watering

Daily check in summer — soil must stay consistently moist, never waterlogged, never dry. Wind at 12th floor dries soil far faster than expected; do not rely on visual inspection alone — check by touch 5 cm down. Every 3–4 days in spring and autumn. Weekly in winter on frost-free days only. Never water onto frozen soil.

## Feeding

Ericaceous (acid-loving) slow-release fertilizer in April only. Do not feed after July — promotes soft late growth that is vulnerable to early frosts. If leaves begin to yellow between the veins (interveinal chlorosis), apply Sequestrene iron tonic immediately — this indicates pH creep above 6.5.

## Winter Care

Hardy to -20°C in ground. In a pot, roots experience temperatures approximately 10°C colder than air temperature — effective root exposure is down to -10°C on the coldest Bucharest nights. Wrap pot heavily in bubble wrap plus an outer layer of fleece. Group tightly with other pots against the building wall. On nights forecast below -15°C, add additional fleece over the canopy. Do not prune in autumn.

## Additional Notes

**NEVER use standard Kekkilä universal mix** — the higher pH causes immediate iron chlorosis. Group A acidic mix is non-negotiable for this plant.

**THE PRIMARY THREAT IS WIND.** Young magnolia leaves and emerging flower buds are thin and delicate. Even 40 km/h gusts will shred them. The most wind-sheltered Z-corner position available is essential. There is no mitigation for chronic wind exposure — placement is everything.

Blooms on bare wood April–May, before leaves emerge. Late Bucharest frosts are common until mid-April and will brown the flowers — this is cosmetic damage only, not fatal to the plant. If a late frost is forecast while flower buds are forming or open, drape a single layer of horticultural fleece over the canopy overnight and remove in the morning.

Use a fabric inner pot and pot up once into this final size, then leave it permanently. Magnolias intensely resent root disturbance — repotting a second time risks killing the plant. The fabric pot allows clean removal if absolutely necessary without damaging the root ball.

'Susan' is one of the Little Girl hybrid series, bred for compact habit and cold hardiness. Flowers are deep reddish-purple, tulip-shaped, appearing over several weeks in spring with occasional repeat blooms in summer.
