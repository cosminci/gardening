# Blue Fescue (*Festuca glauca*)

**Tier:** 1 | **Category:** Grass | **Survival estimate:** 97% | **Quantity:** 3

## Placement
Sun edges. Evergreen, loves wind, silver-blue color. Beautiful ornamental grass that thrives in exposed positions.

## Pot Setup

| | Type | Volume | Dimensions (approx.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inner pot** | Plastic | 5L | ~20cm Ø × 18cm H |
| **Outer pot** | Rigid, light-colored, no drain holes | 10L | ~27cm Ø × 22cm H |

**System:** Shared tray + capillary mat (ideal for grouping)

## Soil Mix — Group B: Mediterranean

| Component | Ratio | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| Kekkilä universal | 40% | 1.3L |
| Perlite / pumice | 30% | 1.0L |
| Coarse sand / gravel | 20% | 0.7L |
| LECA (in mix) | 10% | 0.3L |
| **Total soil mix** | | **3.3L** |

### Pot layers (bottom to top)
1. **Drainage** — LECA: 0.8L
2. **Transition** — pure perlite: 0.3L
3. **Soil mix**: 3.3L
4. **Mulch** — 2-3cm gravel (Mediterranean plants prefer mineral mulch)

## Watering
- **Summer:** Every 3-4 days. Drought-tolerant — let dry between waterings.
- **Winter:** Every 2-3 weeks on frost-free days.

## Feeding
- Slow-release granular in March. No other feeding needed — thrives in poor soil.

## Winter Care
- Hardy to -30°C+. Wrap pot. Evergreen — stays silver-blue year-round.
- Cut back old foliage in late February to encourage fresh growth.

## Additional Notes
- Moves beautifully in wind — ornamental value even in winter.
- Compact clump form, 20-30cm tall. Replace every 3-4 years when center dies out (normal for fescues).
- ×3 plants creates a nice grouping along sunny edges.
