# Rugosa Rose (*Rosa rugosa*)

**Tier:** 2 | **Category:** Shrub | **Survival estimate:** 85% | **Quantity:** 1

## Placement

Full sun. The toughest rose species available — handles intense wind, cold, and salt spray. Does not need sheltering. Well-suited to the exposed S-W orientation and 12th-floor wind.

## Watering

Every 2 days in summer. Every 3–4 days in spring and autumn. Weekly in winter. Rosa rugosa is more drought-tolerant than hybrid roses but still benefits from consistent moisture during flowering.

## Feeding

Rose-specific slow-release fertilizer in March. Liquid high-potash feed every 2 weeks May–August to support continuous flowering. Avoid high-nitrogen feed — promotes lush soft growth that is wind-vulnerable and disease-prone.

## Winter Care

Hardy to -30°C — one of the most cold-tolerant roses. Deciduous. Wrap pot in bubble wrap November–March. Do not prune in autumn — old stems provide wind protection for the crown. Prune in March, cutting back to healthy outward-facing buds.

## Additional Notes

Fragrant flowers June–September (repeat-blooming), followed by large, round, deep-red rose hips that are edible and vitamin C-rich. Disease-resistant — unlike hybrid teas, does not require fungicide sprays. The dense thorny habit makes it a practical deterrent against the pot being disturbed. Salt and wind tolerant by nature — a coast-native plant. Wrinkled (rugose) leaves are characteristic and give the species its name. An excellent low-maintenance choice for a high-exposure balcony.
