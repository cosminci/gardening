# Mexican Feather Grass (*Stipa tenuissima*)

**Tier:** 1 | **Category:** Grass | **Survival estimate:** 93% | **Quantity:** 2

## Placement
Sun zones. Moves beautifully in wind, drought-proof. One of the most beautiful wind-tolerant grasses available.

## Pot Setup

| | Type | Volume | Dimensions (approx.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inner pot** | Plastic | 8L | ~24cm Ø × 20cm H |
| **Outer pot** | Rigid, light-colored, no drain holes | 12L | ~28cm Ø × 24cm H |

**System:** Shared tray + capillary mat

### Pot layers (bottom to top)
1. **Drainage** — LECA: 1.2L
2. **Transition** — pure perlite: 0.4L
3. **Soil mix**: 5.2L
4. **Mulch** — 2-3cm gravel (Mediterranean plants prefer mineral mulch)

## Watering
- **Summer:** Every 3-4 days. Very drought tolerant — let dry between waterings.
- **Winter:** Every 2-3 weeks on frost-free days.

## Feeding
- Minimal. Slow-release granular in March only. Thrives in poor soil — excess feeding reduces ornamental quality.

## Winter Care
- Hardy to -20°C. Wrap pot. Cut back in late February to encourage fresh growth.
- Semi-evergreen — may retain some foliage through mild winters.

## Additional Notes
- 40-60cm tall wispy blonde grass. Mesmerizing movement in even the lightest breeze.
- Self-seeds readily — can spread to neighboring pots. Remove unwanted seedlings early.
- ×2 plants creates a flowing, naturalistic effect in sun zones.
