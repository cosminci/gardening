# Stonecrop (*Sedum spectabile*)

**Tier:** 1 | **Category:** Perennial (succulent) | **Survival estimate:** 98% | **Quantity:** 3

## Placement
Full sun. Indestructible succulent — one of the most reliable plants for an exposed, hot balcony.

## Pot Setup

| | Type | Volume | Dimensions (approx.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inner pot** | Plastic | 5L | ~20cm Ø × 18cm H |
| **Outer pot** | Rigid, light-colored, no drain holes | 10L | ~27cm Ø × 22cm H |

**System:** Shared tray + capillary mat

## Soil Mix — Group B: Mediterranean

| Component | Ratio | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| Kekkilä universal | 40% | 1.3L |
| Perlite / pumice | 30% | 1.0L |
| Coarse sand / gravel | 20% | 0.7L |
| LECA (in mix) | 10% | 0.3L |
| **Total soil mix** | | **3.3L** |

### Pot layers (bottom to top)
1. **Drainage** — LECA: 0.8L
2. **Transition** — pure perlite: 0.3L
3. **Soil mix**: 3.3L
4. **Mulch** — 2-3cm gravel (Mediterranean plants prefer mineral mulch)

## Watering
- **Summer:** Every 4-5 days. Succulent — stores water in leaves. Let soil dry out fully between waterings.
- **Winter:** Monthly at most. Overwatering during dormancy is the primary cause of death.

## Feeding
- None needed. Thrives in poor soil — excess feeding produces weak, floppy growth.

## Winter Care
- Hardy to -30°C. Dies back completely to ground level — do not panic, this is normal.
- Leave dead flower heads through winter for structural interest and wildlife value.
- Re-emerges reliably from the rootstock in spring.

## Additional Notes
- Pink/red flat-topped flower heads in late summer (August–September), beloved by bees and butterflies.
- 30-50cm tall when in growth. Virtually indestructible in the right conditions.
- ×3 plants provides a strong late-summer display and excellent pollinator value.
