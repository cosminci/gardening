# Dwarf Mountain Pine (*Pinus mugo*)

**Tier:** 1 | **Category:** Evergreen Structure | **Survival estimate:** 98%

## Placement
Full-sun scorching spot — the primary "big plant" anchor. Alpine native bred for wind, extreme heat, and extreme cold. Position at the windward edge where it can also act as a windbreak for more delicate plants behind it. S-W direct sun all day is ideal.

## Pot Setup

| | Type | Volume | Dimensions (approx.) |

### Pot layers (bottom to top)
1. **Drainage** — LECA: 9L
2. **Transition** — pure perlite: 3L
3. **Soil mix**: 39L
4. **Mulch** — 3–4cm gravel (helps reflect heat and suppress evaporation)

## Watering
- **Summer (Jun–Aug):** Every 2–3 days. Check reservoir — refill before it runs dry. Drought-tolerant once established but container limits buffer.
- **Spring / Autumn:** Every 5–7 days, reduce as temperatures drop.
- **Winter:** Every 10–14 days only when above freezing. No watering on frozen days.

## Feeding
Slow-release granular conifer fertilizer in March only — push into soil surface. No liquid feed needed. Over-fertilizing promotes soft growth that is more wind-vulnerable.

## Winter Care
Hardy to -35°C — the plant itself needs zero protection. Wrap only the outer pot in jute/bubble wrap to prevent freeze-thaw cracking. Keep reservoir empty from November through February to prevent ice damage.

## Additional Notes
Grows to 1–1.5m in container. Extremely low maintenance — essentially plant-and-forget. Use strategically as a windbreak on the windward side; plants behind it will experience significantly less wind stress. Needs no pruning beyond removing dead branches. Very slow-growing — one purchase lasts decades. Alternative: Juniperus chinensis 'Stricta' (columnar form, much smaller footprint, same hardiness).
