# Hens & Chicks (*Sempervivum* spp.)

**Tier:** 1 | **Category:** Perennial (succulent) | **Survival estimate:** 99% | **Quantity:** 5

## Placement
Edges, crevices, anywhere with sun. Literally survives everything — the most resilient plant available for this terrace.

## Pot Setup

| | Type | Volume | Dimensions (approx.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inner pot** | Plastic | 3L | ~16cm Ø × 14cm H |
| **Outer pot** | Rigid, light-colored, no drain holes | 5L | ~20cm Ø × 18cm H |

**System:** Shared tray or standalone small pots

## Soil Mix — Group B: Mediterranean

| Component | Ratio | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| Kekkilä universal | 40% | 0.8L |
| Perlite / pumice | 30% | 0.6L |
| Coarse sand / gravel | 20% | 0.4L |
| LECA (in mix) | 10% | 0.2L |
| **Total soil mix** | | **2.0L** |

### Pot layers (bottom to top)
1. **Drainage** — LECA: 0.5L
2. **Transition** — pure perlite: 0.2L
3. **Soil mix**: 2.0L
4. **Mulch** — 2-3cm grit or fine gravel (keeps crown dry, prevents rot)

## Watering
- **Summer:** Weekly at most. Succulent — rot from overwatering is the only real risk.
- **Winter:** Nearly never. Natural rainfall is sufficient. Do not water during freeze periods.

## Feeding
- Never. Sempervivum actively prefers starvation — rich soil causes rot and loss of compact form.

## Winter Care
- Hardy to -40°C. No protection needed for the plant itself.
- Wrap the pot to prevent freeze-thaw damage to the container.
- Foliage may flatten or discolor in hard frost — it will recover fully in spring.

## Additional Notes
- Multiplies by offsets (the "chicks") — will fill the pot within a season.
- Available in a huge range of colors: green, red, purple, bronze, silver. Mix varieties for visual interest.
- Can be planted in gaps and crevices between larger pots — excellent for filling dead space.
- The ultimate "plant it and forget it" option. Neglect is genuinely beneficial.
- ×5 plants across small pots creates a varied, low-maintenance tapestry effect.
