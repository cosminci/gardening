# Russian Vine (*Fallopia baldschuanica*)

**Tier:** 1 | **Category:** Climber | **Survival estimate:** 95%

## Placement
Any position where fast, aggressive coverage is the goal. This plant handles everything — full sun, deep shade, wind, drought, cold. Best deployed where you need maximum coverage in minimum time, particularly on unattractive walls or as a privacy screen. Needs a strong support structure anchored to the wall.

## Watering
- **Summer (Jun–Aug):** Every 2 days. High growth rate means high water demand.
- **Spring / Autumn:** Every 4–5 days.
- **Winter:** Weekly when above freezing. Deciduous — reduce significantly after leaf drop.

## Feeding
Slow-release granular in March. Liquid feeding is optional — this plant grows aggressively regardless. Over-feeding makes it even harder to control.

## Winter Care
Essentially indestructible in cold. Hardy well below -20°C. Deciduous — goes fully dormant and looks like a collection of bare twigs. Wrap outer pot. Plant will return vigorously each spring regardless of winter severity.

## Additional Notes
Can grow 4–5m per year — earned the nickname "mile-a-minute vine." This is its primary selling point and its primary challenge. Plan the support structure before planting — it needs something that can hold significant weight once mature. Annual hard pruning in late winter (February) keeps it manageable and actually stimulates better growth. Deciduous, so provides no winter screening. White/pale pink flowers appear in late summer, mildly ornamental. Once established, essentially unstoppable — choose its position carefully.
