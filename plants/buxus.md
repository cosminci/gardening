# Boxwood (*Buxus sempervirens*)

**Tier:** 1 | **Category:** Evergreen Structure | **Survival estimate:** 95%

## Placement
Partial shade to full sun. Classic container evergreen — tolerates the full range of light conditions on the terrace. Particularly good in the transition zone between the shaded Z-corner and the exposed sunny areas. Wind-tolerant and structurally reliable year-round.

## Watering
- **Summer (Jun–Aug):** Every 2–3 days. Consistent moisture is important — buxus dislikes both drought and waterlogging.
- **Spring / Autumn:** Every 5–7 days.
- **Winter:** Weekly when above freezing.

## Feeding
- **March:** Slow-release granular fertilizer applied to soil surface.
- **May–July:** Light liquid feed (balanced NPK) every 3–4 weeks for denser, more compact growth. Optional but improves foliage density.

## Winter Care
Hardy to -20°C and beyond. Plant needs no special protection. Wrap outer pot. Maintain light watering through winter — buxus is evergreen and continues slow transpiration even in cold. Avoid letting roots freeze completely dry.

## Additional Notes
Can be clipped into any shape — spheres, cones, cubes, or left as natural rounded mounds. Pruning best done in late spring (May) and again lightly in August. Box blight (Cylindrocladium) is the main disease risk in sheltered, humid conditions — however, the strong wind on this 12th-floor terrace provides excellent natural air circulation that significantly reduces fungal risk. If blight appears, remove affected stems immediately and improve spacing.
