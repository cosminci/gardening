# Creeping Juniper (*Juniperus horizontalis*) ×2

**Tier:** 1 | **Category:** Evergreen Structure | **Survival estimate:** 97%

## Placement
Sun zones along edges of the terrace. Low-growing and spreading, ideal for pot edges and exposed corners where upright plants would suffer wind rock. Wind-proof and drought-proof. Two plants recommended — one per sunny edge zone.

## Pot Setup

| | Type | Volume | Dimensions (approx.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inner pot** | Plastic | 15L | ~Ø30cm x H25cm |
| **Outer pot** | Rigid, light-colored, no drain holes | 25L | ~Ø35cm x H30cm |

**System:** Double-wall or shared tray

## Soil Mix — Group D: Conifers/Evergreens

| Component | Ratio | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| Kekkilä universal | 60% | 6.0L |
| Perlite / expanded clay | 25% | 2.5L |
| Composted bark | 15% | 1.5L |
| **Total soil mix** | | **10.0L** |

### Pot layers (bottom to top)
1. **Drainage** — LECA: 2.3L
2. **Transition** — pure perlite: 0.8L
3. **Soil mix**: 10L
4. **Mulch** — 3cm gravel

## Watering
- **Summer (Jun–Aug):** Every 3–4 days. Very drought-tolerant; err on the side of dry rather than wet.
- **Spring / Autumn:** Every 7–10 days.
- **Winter:** Every 2–3 weeks only when above freezing.

## Feeding
Slow-release granular conifer fertilizer in March only. Push lightly into soil surface. No additional feeding required.

## Winter Care
Hardy to -35°C. Plant is fully self-sufficient in cold. Wrap outer pot in jute or bubble wrap to prevent cracking. Drain reservoir through November–February.

## Additional Notes
Stays green year-round — provides winter structure when most other plants are bare. Low-growing and spreading habit makes it excellent for pot edges and as a natural visual wind buffer at ground level. Very forgiving of neglect. Grows slowly enough that a 15L pot suits it for many years before repotting is needed.
