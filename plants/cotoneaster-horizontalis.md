# Cotoneaster horizontalis (*Cotoneaster horizontalis*)

**Tier:** 2 | **Category:** Shrub | **Survival estimate:** 85% | **Quantity:** 1

## Placement

Sun to partial shade. Thrives in both exposed and sheltered positions. The naturally low, spreading form makes it ideal for trailing over pot edges or as a front-of-arrangement groundcover. Suits any position on the balcony.

## Watering

Every 2–3 days in summer. Every 5–7 days in spring and autumn. Every 2 weeks in winter — very drought-tolerant once established, but do not let roots desiccate completely.

## Feeding

Slow-release balanced granular fertilizer in March only. No additional feeding required.

## Winter Care

Hardy to -25°C. Semi-evergreen — retains small dark green leaves in mild winters, drops them in harder winters. Bright red berries persist well into winter, providing color and food for birds. Wrap pot in bubble wrap November–March. No pruning needed; the natural herringbone form should not be cut — it does not regenerate well from hard pruning.

## Additional Notes

The distinctive flat herringbone branching pattern is architecturally striking and looks particularly good draped over pot edges or trained against a wall. Berries attract birds from autumn through winter — a welcome bit of wildlife on a high-floor balcony. Extremely tough and reliable; tolerates pollution, wind, and a wide range of soil conditions. One of the most resilient container shrubs available for USDA Zone 6b. A structural, low-effort plant that earns its place year-round.
