# Juniperus squamata 'Blue Star' (*Juniperus squamata* 'Blue Star')

**Tier:** 1 | **Category:** Evergreen Structure | **Survival estimate:** 96%

## Placement
Sun to partial shade. Very compact and dense — suited to a featured spot where the silver-blue foliage color provides year-round visual interest. Wind-resistant due to low, mounded habit. Works well near the Pinus mugo or in a cluster with other compact evergreens.

## Watering
- **Summer (Jun–Aug):** Every 3–4 days. Drought-tolerant; avoid overwatering which is the main risk.
- **Spring / Autumn:** Every 7–10 days.
- **Winter:** Every 2–3 weeks only when above freezing.

## Feeding
Slow-release granular conifer fertilizer in March only. No additional feeding. Over-feeding loses the compact, dense habit that makes this cultivar distinctive.

## Winter Care
Hardy to -30°C. Plant needs no protection. Wrap outer pot in insulating material. Drain reservoir November–February.

## Additional Notes
Very compact — grows only 30–50cm tall and wide in 10+ years. Beautiful silver-blue needled foliage that holds color year-round. Minimal pruning needed — remove only dead material. The mounded shape is natural and should not be forced into a different form. Long-lived in containers with minimal care.
