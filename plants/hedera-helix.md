# English Ivy (*Hedera helix*)

**Tier:** 1 | **Category:** Climber | **Survival estimate:** 95%

## Placement
Shaded inner Z-corner ONLY. Direct S-W sun will scorch and kill the leaves — do not expose to afternoon sun. The protected Z-corner pocket with indirect or morning light is the correct position. Can climb a trellis or wire attached to the shaded wall face.

## Watering
Keep consistently moist but never waterlogged. Ivy is the most moisture-sensitive of the Tier 1 plants — it suffers from both drought and root rot.
- **Summer (Jun–Aug):** Every 2–3 days. During heat waves above 35°C, mist the leaves in the morning (not evening) to cool foliage and raise local humidity.
- **Spring / Autumn:** Every 4–5 days.
- **Winter:** Weekly. Evergreen — continues to need moisture through winter.

## Feeding
Liquid fertilizer at half-strength, monthly from April through September. Balanced NPK or slightly higher N to support leafy growth. No feeding October–March.

## Winter Care
Hardy to -20°C. Plant is fully self-sufficient in cold if kept in the sheltered Z-corner. Wrap only the outer pot. Being evergreen, it continues slow growth even in mild winter spells — check soil moisture monthly.
