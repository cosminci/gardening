# Hydrangea paniculata (*Hydrangea paniculata*)

**Tier:** 2 | **Category:** Shrub | **Survival estimate:** 82% | **Quantity:** 1

## Placement

Partial shade. Needs wind protection — place in the sheltered Z-corner. Deciduous; bare in winter. Tolerates more sun than other hydrangea species. White/pink cone-shaped flower heads July–September.

## Watering

Daily in summer — heavy drinker, wilts dramatically when thirsty but recovers quickly once watered. Every 3–4 days in spring and autumn. Weekly in winter. Check reservoir level daily during heat waves (38–42°C).

## Feeding

Slow-release granular fertilizer in March. Liquid balanced fertilizer every 2 weeks May–August.

## Winter Care

Hardy to -25°C in ground. In pot, roots are significantly more exposed — wrap pot heavily in bubble wrap and fleece. Deciduous; prune in late February, cutting back to healthy buds. No autumn pruning. Group with other pots against building wall.

## Additional Notes

Hydrangea paniculata is hardier and more sun-tolerant than the common H. macrophylla. The Z-corner is ideal — provides critical wind shelter for the large flower heads, which snap in strong gusts. Flower heads emerge white in July, age to pink and then papery buff by September; dried heads persist attractively through winter and provide some frost protection for buds below. In a 12th-floor S-W exposure, the sheltered Z-corner is essential — do not place in full wind exposure.
