# Euonymus fortunei 'Emerald Gaiety' (*Euonymus fortunei*)

**Tier:** 2 | **Category:** Evergreen shrub / groundcover | **Survival estimate:** 85% | **Quantity:** 1

## Placement

Partial shade. Extremely versatile — can function as low groundcover around base of larger pots, as a trailing plant over pot edges, or trained up a trellis as a climber. Good filler in the shadier Z-corner sections.

## Watering

Every 2–3 days in summer. Every 5–7 days in spring and autumn. Weekly in winter — do not let evergreen roots freeze dry.

## Feeding

Slow-release balanced granular fertilizer in March only. No additional liquid feeding required.

## Winter Care

Hardy to -25°C. Evergreen — retains its variegated green and white leaves year-round. In cold temperatures the white margins take on a pink-rose flush, adding winter interest. Wrap pot in bubble wrap November–March. No pruning required; trim any wayward shoots in spring if needed.

## Additional Notes

'Emerald Gaiety' is a compact cultivar with bright white-edged leaves that reflect light well in shaded positions. Very low maintenance once established. The self-clinging stems can attach to walls or trellis without support ties. In containers, combines well as an underplanting beneath larger shrubs or as a standalone trailing specimen. Reliable, unfussy, and provides year-round foliage color — an excellent structural filler plant for the balcony.
